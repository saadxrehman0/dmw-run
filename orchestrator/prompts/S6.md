## S6 Next edition prep (after 10:20)

Task files: task-03-calendar-file and task-04-writeup-handoff; the monthly names check from
task-00-daily-run on the first run of a month (quiet window, after the edition is out).
Build the next edition's ({NEXT_EDITION}) Manual File-Calendar.xlsx and write-up into
{RUN}/practice/next/ (practice) or the next edition's day folder (live, guarded). Conflicts open
gates; they are answered before the next run.

Calendar rows: do not write fetch scripts of your own. One call reads the Saudi Exchange dividends,
general assembly and corporate actions tabs (Main Market and Nomu), the assembly announcements and
the investing.com US and KSA calendar in its own visible browser window (about 2 minutes), and
applies calendar.md's day, tier, figure and wording rules (<next> = {RUN}/practice/next in practice,
{RUN}/next live):

    {PY} {REPO}/scripts/cal_fetch.py --edition {NEXT_EDITION} --after {EDITION} --out "<next>/cal.json" --rows "<next>/rows.json" --names "<state Mapping.xlsx, rules/config.md>" --prev-cal "{DAY}/Manual File-Calendar.xlsx"

In a replay add `--announced-before {EDITION}` (dividends announced later were not public yet).
Exit 1 means a site could not be read (no Saudi route, or blocked): the rest of cal.json still
holds what was read; say which site in the result and gate the calendar.
Then work through cal.json:
1. `review`: settle every item. A TK figure comes from the linked announcement or stays TK with a
   conflict gate; a TK Arabic wording comes from the latest final AR deck (slide 2), and a new
   economic event's wording goes into {REPO}/data/cal_wording.json only through a gate (practice:
   note it in the result). A corporate-actions cross-check item becomes a row only when its
   eligibility date is the edition's and the event block lacks it.
2. The "by hand" item: the Argaam calendar for earnings calls, listing and index events and KSA
   releases investing.com lacks (task-03 steps 2 and 3). Add those rows to rows.json in the
   house wording; log their source.
3. Keep `sources` (one line per row) as the source log; add your own rows' lines.
Then run build_cal.py and fix_cal_ar_names.py as task-03 step 5 says, and check_ar_text.py on the
Arabic you wrote.
